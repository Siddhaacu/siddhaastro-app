Privacy Policy

How Siddha Astro handles information when you use the app.

1. Information and preferences

App preferences such as language, theme, city, text size and astrology preferences may be stored on your device so Siddha Astro can remember your choices.

2. Account and sign-in

If you choose to sign in, authentication is provided through Firebase and Google sign-in. Information made available by the sign-in provider is used to support your account experience.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app may use a notification subscription and device or browser permission to deliver supported updates. You can manage notification permissions through your device or browser settings.

4. Personal Events

Personal Event entries created in the current app version are stored in local browser storage on the device where they are added. This feature does not currently synchronise events between devices.

5. Your choices

You can change saved preferences in Settings. Clearing Siddha Astro site data may remove locally stored preferences and Personal Events. Keep your device and account credentials secure.

6. Policy updates

This policy may be revised as app features change. The version displayed above identifies the document revision shown in Siddha Astro.

Siddha Astro Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This notice explains the account information Siddha Astro stores when you sign in with Google.

Information collected

When you sign in, Siddha Astro receives and stores your Google account identifier, display name, email address, profile photo URL (if provided), sign-in provider, account creation timestamp, last sign-in timestamp and last activity timestamp. The app does not receive or store your Google password.

How the information is used

Account information is used to authenticate you, maintain your profile, support account administration and prepare a confidential weekly user report for the Siddha Astro administrator at siddhagroupss@gmail.com. The report includes all account profiles held in the user-report database, with registration and recent activity summaries.

Sharing and security

The report is sent to the designated administrator using a server-side email service. Administrative credentials are stored as deployment secrets and are not exposed in the app. Account information is not intended for sale or public disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Your profile remains stored while your Siddha Astro account is active. You can request deletion using the Delete Account option in My Profile. Deletion removes the profile record and initiates deletion of the Firebase Authentication account; certain records may remain temporarily in backups or service logs.

Your choices and contact

You may choose not to sign in. If you have questions or want to request access, correction or deletion of your account information, contact siddhagroupss@gmail.com.

Use of Siddha Astro is subject to applicable privacy and data-protection laws.

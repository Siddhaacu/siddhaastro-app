How it is calculated

Traditional Navatara and Moon-sign counting.

  • Tarabalam counts from your Janma Nakshatra to the day's Moon Nakshatra and reduces the count to a 1–9 Tara cycle.
  • Sampat, Kshema, Sadhana, Mitra and Parama Mitra are generally favourable; Vipat, Pratyari and Naidhana are cautionary. Janma is mixed.
  • Chandrabalam counts the day's Moon sign from your Janma Rashi. The 1st, 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th and 11th positions are traditionally strong.
